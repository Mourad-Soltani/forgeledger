# ForgeLedger sales kit — Mourad.Soltani

## Offer

ForgeLedger is a freelance command center: clients, invoices, expenses, proposal drafts. Built to close higher-quality clients, not to replace a full ERP.

## Ideal customer profile

1. Boutique agencies (web, brand, AI implementation) billing $8k–$40k projects
2. Senior freelancers who still send Word proposals
3. Studio operators who lose 4–6 hours/month reconciling invoices

## Pricing to close best clients

| Tier | Price | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Solo | $29 / month | one operator |
| Studio | $79 / month | 2–8 people |
| Founder license | $1,490 one-time | studios that want source + white-label |

Do not discount below 20%. Prefer annual (2 months free) over cheap monthly.

## Outreach sequence (not bulk)

1. Identify a studio that just hired or just launched a service page.
2. Send one short email referencing a specific public project.
3. Offer a 12-minute teardown of their last invoice / proposal flow.
4. If they reply, generate a ForgeLedger proposal *using the product* and attach it.

## Objection handling

- "We use QuickBooks" → ForgeLedger is the front-of-house for proposals and project invoices; QBO stays the ledger of record.
- "AI writers already exist" → buyers pay for a numbered commercial artifact with totals and status, not another chat window.
- "Too small" → that is the point. 200 studios at $79 is a real business.

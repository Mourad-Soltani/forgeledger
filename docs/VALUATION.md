## Acquisition value *today* (pre-revenue)

Marketplaces discount heavily without MRR. Pre-revenue / early MVP comps:

| Scenario | Indicative range | Buyer |
|---|---|---|
| Code + docs only, no traction | **$3k–$12k** | Indie flippers, SideProjectors-style |
| Polished product + demo + Docker + license system | **$8k–$25k** | Operators who want a head start |
| + early LOIs or 5–20 paying pilots | **$25k–$60k** | Micro-acquirers |

Median small SaaS asks on marketplaces cluster around **~2× trailing revenue** or **~3.4× profit** once revenue exists — not relevant until MRR is real.

## Who buys ForgeLedger

**Now (asset / early)**  
- Solo operators wanting a billing + proposal product to white-label  
- Agencies buying a tool for *their* freelancers (studio license)  
- Micro-SaaS flippers (Acquire.com / Flippa / Microns once MRR appears)

**Later (with MRR)**  
- Searchers / individual buyers under $500k deal size  
- Adjacent product companies (proposal tools, CRM lite) for feature tuck-in  

**Best commercial path (not pure flip)**  
1. Founding licenses at **$1,490** (5–15 units) → $7k–$22k cash  
2. Convert to **$29 / $79** SaaS  
3. Sell the *operating* business at 3–4× profit after 12–18 months of retention proof  

## Honest ceiling / floor

- **Floor (today):** a few thousand USD if sold as source-only.  
- **Realistic near-term (3–6 mo with 30–80 paying):** **$40k–$150k** acquisition ask.  
- **Do not** list at VC-style 8–12× ARR — micro marketplace buyers will ignore it.
